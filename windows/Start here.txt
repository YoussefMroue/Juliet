Proposal Review | Windows 11 pilot

Keep the application folder together. Open Proposal Review.exe through your
company's approved software process. The folder includes its own runtime.

First practice run
1. Create a proposal called Practice. Open Practice cases, then clean. In the app, choose input.xlsx as the budget
   and input.docx as the justification.
2. Inspect the rules. For this fictional pair, replace Mira Example with Person 01,
   mira@example.invalid with Email 01, XQ-417 with ID 01, and MX with Initials 01.
   Choose Redact term or Keep selected for each suggestion. Check the hidden sheet and comments.
3. Inspect both copies, approve saving the round, then select Open saved round.
4. Open Budget.xlsx and Justification.docx from its 03 Redacted folder. Confirm
   the total is 900, the formula still works, and the labels agree across files.
5. The Review kit folder contains the intake, review instructions, findings
   template and expected results for all five fictional practice cases.
