Government guidelines


Reference list for manual review. The app currently inspects and redacts documents. It does not apply these sources as automated compliance rules or establish compliance. This starter list will expand when the full requirements are supplied.


Links checked November 3, 2026. These official publisher pages point to current guidance; the applicable edition depends on the opportunity, due date, recipient and award. Recheck updates before each review. The app does not refresh guidance automatically.


2 CFR Part 200, Subpart E — Cost Principles


Federal cost principles, including allowability, reasonableness and allocation (§§ 200.403–200.405). Confirm applicability and exceptions under § 200.401 and the agency’s award terms; this is not a universal proposal checklist. The eCFR is authoritative but is not the official legal edition.


Version observed: Title 2 current through October 30, 2026; last amended August 17, 2026.


NIH — How to Apply: Application Guide


Form and attachment instructions. Choose the instructions for the activity code and due date, then check the specific funding opportunity and applicable NIH notices.


Version observed: SF424 (R&R), Version I, for due dates on or after January 25, 2025; general instructions updated December 2025.


NIH — Develop Your Budget


Budget preparation guidance covering detailed and modular budgets, justifications and cost considerations. These tips do not supersede the applicable application instructions. Verify ceilings, salary limits and rates for the actual opportunity and period; no amounts are built into this app.


Version: living guidance page checked November 3, 2026.


Requirements still needed for each proposal


Keep the solicitation or funding opportunity, amendments, applicable notices and exact budget sections in the round’s 02 Guidance folder. Record versions, effective dates, direct versus total cost ceilings and whether each limit covers one period or the full award.


Tulane’s institutional requirements, rates and exceptions must be supplied and confirmed separately. They are not inferred from these federal references. Record missing or conflicting guidance as a question for the reviewer before reaching a determination.
