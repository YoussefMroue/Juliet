# Pilot with Lynn

## Pilot preparation

Windows 11 on a company-managed machine is confirmed. The first walkthrough uses the fictional cases because a previously reviewed, de-identified proposal is currently unavailable. Collect answers from the project owner first. Take only unresolved workflow questions to Lynn and unresolved distribution or data-handling questions to the appropriate company contact.

## Open questions

1. For eventual reviewer acceptance, can Lynn compare the tool’s findings with a previously reviewed case within her approved working environment?
2. Which Office formats are used? Do budgets have protection, macros, links, or comments? Are justifications Word files, PDFs, or scans?
3. What must be removed, and what must stay legible? Consider names, initials, email, IDs, signatures, roles, salaries and effort. Is a consistent personnel label acceptable across files?
4. Who can confirm the permitted workspace, which materials may leave the computer, and where approved copies may be retained? Include unpublished proposal details and internal guidance.
5. Where do you obtain controlling sponsor and federal guidance, amendments and effective dates? How do you check that a solicitation number identifies the right version?
6. Which Tulane rules apply universally, and what changes by grant? Give one prohibited cost, one exception and one judgment call.
7. Which cross-document checks and report citations do you need? Show one correction you would send back and one clarification you would ask first.

## Synthetic review exercises

The following practice rules and figures are fictional. Use the generator described in the README to create a separate Office pair and guidance file per scenario. Give the reviewer only the exercise inputs, intake and review instructions. Keep the answer key here until scoring. For the exercise, select the grand total and budget category as cross-checks. No institutional exceptions are supplied.

| Exercise | Expected result | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| clean | No correction supported. | Budget!B4 totals 900. Justification paragraph 2 agrees. Exercise Guidelines §1 allows up to 1,000 total, §2 allows supplies. |
| ceiling | Reduce the request by at least 100 or seek an authorized change. | Budget!B4 totals 1,100. Paragraph 2 agrees. Cite Exercise Guidelines §1, “Total costs may not exceed 1,000.” |
| cost | Remove or replace the 200 entertainment cost. | Budget!A3:B3 and paragraph 2. Cite Exercise Guidelines §2, “Entertainment is unallowable.” |
| missing | Request the governing text and version before reviewing. | Only the opportunity number is supplied. No supported correction. |
| conflict | Ask Lynn which ceiling governs. | Budget!B4 totals 900. §1 gives a 1,000 ceiling and Addendum A gives 800 without stating precedence. Report both passages. |

## Known-case acceptance

Run one previously reviewed, de-identified case with Lynn. Keep her historical corrections aside until the report is complete. Compare each reported location, citation, amount and proposed fix against her review. Record missed issues, false alarms and any incorrect citations. Revise the instructions and repeat the case. Before using real proposals, record Lynn’s acceptance and institutional confirmation of the handoff and storage rules outside this repository.
